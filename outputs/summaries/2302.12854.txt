Existing Summary:
- Objective:
  - To introduce the concept of the Micro-Paper as a cheaper and citable way to present late-breaking research ideas and conversations.
  
- Methods:
  - The Micro-Paper is a digital document between 1 and 4 pages in length that engages a single idea clearly.
  - It is self-published and archived, similar to a pre-print of a short paper, and is published through an open archive.
  
- Results:
  - The Micro-Paper fills a gap between fast, cheap ideas and rigorous, archival work.
  - It allows for the dissemination of raw, unrefined research ideas in a cheap and accessible manner.
  
- Limitations:
  - The Micro-Paper format may not be suitable for all types of research or academic communities.
  
- Future Work:
  - Further exploration of the effectiveness and reception of the Micro-Paper format in different academic fields and communities.
